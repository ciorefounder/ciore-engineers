Build With Cioré Across Hardware, Software, and Power

Cioré connects a uniquely designed smartphone, an open source Android operating system, and hybrid charging hardware. We are looking for three types of partners: teams who can build functional prototypes, specialists who can test feasibility, and advisors who can point us toward scaling partners.

The Program

One connected product, built around ownership

Four goals guide every engineering decision, from the body in your hand to how it charges.

Body
01

User Assembled

A seamless body crafted with smooth curves and flush sensor controls, designed to arrive as prepared modular parts anyone can assemble without soldering or factory tools.

Longevity
02

Built for Repair

Internal layouts, eco friendly materials, and healthier alternative components planned so worn parts can be serviced and replaced easily.

Software
03

Open Source Lumé OS

A planned Android Open Source Project (AOSP) operating system with clear privacy controls, accessibility features, and Gaia, an optional tool that explains observable privacy activity (not AI).

Energy
04

Nyro Hybrid Power

Solar assisted phone charging concepts paired with a combined wall charger and power bank featuring foldable prongs and a retractable USB C cable.

Hi, I am Chrissy, founder of Cioré. My love for STEM started in high school when I was part of the robotics team, and today I work at one of the largest healthcare organizations in the world while completing an accelerated program for my master's degree in information technology.

Hardware
01

Phone Hardware & Materials

Turn our uniquely designed, user assembled phone into tested hardware using eco friendly materials and healthier components.

Who Fits
  • Custom PCB boards and smartphone electronics
  • Mechanical design for user assembly, repairability, and flush sensors
  • RF, cellular, and antenna engineering
  • Eco friendly materials and ethical component sourcing
  • Prototyping, verification, compliance, and distribution
Software
02

Lumé OS & Gaia

Build our planned open source Android operating system on reference hardware alongside Gaia’s observable privacy view.

Who Fits
  • AOSP platform engineering beyond standard mobile apps
  • Open source architecture and hardware bring up
  • Mobile security, privacy, and accessibility
  • Ongoing software updates and system maintenance
Energy
03

Nyro Energy & Solar

Validate and build our solar assisted charging concepts alongside our combined wall charger and portable power bank.

Who Fits
  • Solar power products and small photovoltaic collection
  • Power electronics, batteries, and USB C Power Delivery
  • Thermal management and UL or IEC safety certification
  • Energy testing, prototyping, and production prep
